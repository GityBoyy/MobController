# 🐾 Mob Controller Mod

Take control of any mob with the power of the **Mob Controller**! Transform the way you play Minecraft by commanding mobs to move, fight, and perform actions like never before!

---

## 📦 Features

- **👾 Control Any Mob**: Right-click with the Mob Controller to gain control of any mob in the game.
- **🎮 Direct Commands**: Seamlessly use your keyboard controls to move the mob or perform actions.
- **🐴 Ride Mobs**: Hold Shift while right-clicking to ride mobs that are eligible for riding!
- **🔄 Multiplayer Compatibility**: Works in multiplayer, so you can impress (or prank) your friends!

## 🎮 Usage

1. **Craft the Mob Controller**:
   - Gather the required materials to craft the Mob Controller item. *(Recipe coming soon!)*
   - Equip the Mob Controller in your main hand.

2. **Right-Click a Mob**:
   - Approach any mob and right-click it with the Mob Controller.
   - If successful, you will see a burst of particles and hear a sound effect!

3. **Control the Mob**:
   - Use your regular movement keys to control the mob’s movements.
   - Hold Shift and right-click to ride eligible mobs.

4. **Release the Mob**:
   - Simply switch to another item or walk away from the mob to release control.
